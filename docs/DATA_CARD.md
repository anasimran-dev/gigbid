# Data card
- **All data in this repo is synthetic**, generated by `scripts/generate_data.py` (seed 4016): 3 personas (CV, 15 case studies, 5 repos, reviews, 150 past proposals), 340 jobs (incl. scams, prompt injections, vague posts, out-of-domain, 20 duplicates), 132 clients, 1,500 history jobs, and 200 dev-set jobs per persona with gold labels.
- **Real data**: none used. The optional Kaggle datasets were considered but not downloaded (no network in the build environment); to add them, place raw files under `data/real/` and map columns in the generator. If added they must be kept separate from synthetic data, attributed, and used under their licences.
- **GigSim outcomes** come from a hidden-preference simulator I wrote; reply/hire rates measure fit to my simulator, not the real market.
- **Limitations**: synthetic text is templated, so retrieval scores are likely higher than on real portfolios; embeddings are LSA and the reranker is lexical (offline stand-ins).
- **Privacy**: no real personal data; PII masking still applied in traces.
