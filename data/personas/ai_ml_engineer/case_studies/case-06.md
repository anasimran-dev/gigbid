# Demand forecasting for a grocery chain

**Client:** a e-commerce company (synthetic).

## Problem
The client needed predicting next quarter's numbers and had no in-house capacity.

## What I did
I built the solution with Time series, pandas, XGBoost. The work also involved scripting and automation.

## Result
The project reduced forecast error (MAPE) from 22% to 13%.
