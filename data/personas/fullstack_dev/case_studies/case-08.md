# Team chat widget

**Client:** a SaaS company (synthetic).

## Problem
The client needed live updates without refreshing and had no in-house capacity.

## What I did
I built the solution with websocket, nodejs, Redis. The work also involved a snappy single-page interface.

## Result
The project handled 8,000 concurrent connections in load tests.
