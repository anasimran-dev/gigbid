# Pricing experiment readout

**Client:** a media company (synthetic).

## Problem
The client needed knowing whether the new checkout really converts better and had no in-house capacity.

## What I did
I built the solution with ab testing, Statistics, py3. The work also involved self-serve reports for marketing.

## Result
The project showed the higher price raised revenue per user by 11%.
